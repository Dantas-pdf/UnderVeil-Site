Frequently Asked Questions

What is UnderVeil?

UnderVeil is a 2D pixel art roguelike game where you must defeat a maddened antagonist. Each run is procedurally generated, ensuring a unique experience every time you play. With challenging combat, strategic item choices, and meaningful storytelling, we hope to offer hours of immersive gameplay.

How does the roguelike system work?

UnderVeil features a permadeath roguelike system where each run is procedurally generated. When you die, you start fresh with a new run, but you'll earn permanent upgrades and unlock new content that carries over between runs. This creates a rewarding progression system where every attempt brings you closer to mastering the game.

What platforms can I play UnderVeil on?

UnderVeil is currently available only on Windows. We intent to expand to other platforms such as Consoles, Linux, and Mac in the future. But with our budget and experience, we are focusing on delivering the best possible experience on Windows first.

Is UnderVeil free to play?

No, UnderVeil is not free to play. The game will be available for purchase, but we offer a free demo version that allows you to experience a portion of the game before deciding to buy the full version.

How does the pixel art style enhance the gameplay?

Pixel art while not only being less demanding on hardware than high-resolution graphics, it also adds a unique charm and clarity to the gameplay. It allows players to focus on the mechanics and strategy without being distracted by overly complex visuals.

Can I play UnderVeil offline?

Yes, you can play UnderVeil offline! The game is completely offline and doesn't require an internet connection to enjoy the full experience.

Does the game have multiple endings?

Yes, UnderVeil will have multiple endings based on the player's choices. You will have different upgrades, different weapons, different paths, and your own ending.
