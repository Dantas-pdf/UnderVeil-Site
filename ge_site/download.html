Can My PC Run UnderVeil?

Performance is one of our core priorities. UnderVeil is designed to be accessible on a wide range of hardware. Whether you're running a high-end gaming rig or a modest laptop, there's a good chance you can enjoy UnderVeil. We don't want to be like those badly unoptimized games that leave players frustrated without even playing.

Minimum Requirements

The bare minimum to run UnderVeil with playable performance. Expect around 60 FPS.

Recommended Requirements

For an optimal experience with smooth gameplay. 60+ FPS on recommended hardware with these specs.

Check the system requirements below to see where your PC stands, or scroll down to compare your specs.

System Requirements

Minimum Requirements

  • OS: Windows 7 / 8 / 10 (32-bit or 64-bit)
  • Processor: 1.8 GHz dual-core
  • Memory: 2 GB RAM
  • Graphics: DirectX 9 compatible, 128 MB VRAM
  • Storage: 200 MB available space
  • Additional Notes: Keyboard and mouse required

Recommended Requirements

  • OS: Windows 10 (64-bit)
  • Processor: 2.4 GHz dual-core or better
  • Memory: 4 GB RAM
  • Graphics: DirectX 9/11 compatible, 512 MB VRAM
  • Storage: 200 MB available space
  • Additional Notes: Headphones recommended for best audio experience
