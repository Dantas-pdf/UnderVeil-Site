What's UnderVeil?

Discover a dark and mysterious world filled with captivating gameplay and compelling storytelling. UnderVeil combines challenging combat mechanics with an immersive narrative that adapts to your choices.

Every decision matters as you progress through the dungeons, encounter weird characters, fight godlike bosses and put an end to the flame that has been burning for far too long. Whether you're a seasoned gamer or new to the genre, UnderVeil offers an unforgettable experience.

Mythologies of the Veil

Beneath the veil, the old pantheons still listen. UnderVeil brings together multiple mythologies, and each one aids you in its own way, with a different role to play in your journey.

Omens · In-run buffs

Roman Mythology

The Roman gods speak through Omens. Read the signs and claim their favour: each Omen grants a buff that empowers you for the rest of your run, shaping how you fight and survive.

Statues · Permanent progression

Greek Mythology

The Greek gods have fallen silent, their statues lying broken across the hub. Rebuild them to restore your bond with each god and earn permanent upgrades that carry over from run to run.

Why Play UnderVeil?

Dense Story
