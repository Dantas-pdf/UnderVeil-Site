What's UnderVeil?

UnderVeil is a 2D pixel-art roguelike for Windows. You fight your way down through procedurally generated dungeons, and the story moves forward with every run, shaped by the choices you make.

Along the way you'll meet weird characters, face godlike bosses and find more than one ending. The goal stays the same: put an end to the flame that has been burning for far too long.

Mythologies of the Veil

Beneath the veil, the old pantheons still listen. UnderVeil brings together multiple mythologies, and each one aids you in its own way, with a different role to play in your journey.

Roman Mythology

The Roman gods speak through Omens. Read the signs and claim their favour: each Omen grants a buff that empowers you for the rest of your run, shaping how you fight and survive.

Greek Mythology

The Greek gods have fallen silent, their Statues lying broken across the hub. Rebuild them to restore your bond with each god and earn permanent upgrades that carry over from run to run.

More Await…

Rome and Greece are only the beginning. Other pantheons still slumber beneath the veil, waiting to be discovered, each with its own way of aiding those brave enough to seek them out.
